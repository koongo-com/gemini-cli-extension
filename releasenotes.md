Koongo is now available as a Gemini CLI extension - manage your product feeds and marketplace listings by chatting with Gemini.

Koongo is an e-commerce solution that helps online merchants sell everywhere - connecting their product catalogue to 500+ sales channels through product feed management and marketplace integration, all from one place.

**Install:**

    gemini extensions install https://github.com/koongo-com/gemini-cli-extension

**What you can do:**
- Create and configure sales & advertising channel integrations - Google Shopping, Amazon, eBay, Kaufland, Bol, Zalando, Cdiscount and more
- Map channel attributes and store categories, and enrich products with new attributes
- Build, filter, verify and export product feeds
- Track and sync marketplace orders

It connects to the hosted Koongo MCP server at https://mcp.koongo.com/mcp (Streamable HTTP) with secure OAuth sign-in; the extension only accesses the projects your account owns. Anything that publishes to a live channel is behind a confirmation step.
